What this shows, and what it can't

The modes, layouts, button actions, DST rule and leap-second counting match docs/firmware-features.md. The real clock will run the same rules in C.

Your phone's clock is set over the network and can be tens of milliseconds off. The real clock follows the GPS pulse. A screen also redraws only 60–120 times a second, so the last two digits look like a blur, as they will to your eye on the real clock. A camera at a fast shutter speed can read them.

Hardware quirk you can see here: digits 2 and 4 have no decimal point, because their DP outputs drive the colons. When a number's decimal point would land there (for example 1234.5 days), the firmware shifts the number one place right.
